# School Images Directory Guide

Place your school photos and logos in the appropriate folders below:

---

### 1. School Logo
📁 **Folder:** `public/images/logo/`
* **File name:** `logo.png` (or `.jpg`, `.svg`)
* **Usage:** Appears in the top navigation bar and footer.
* **Tip:** A transparent PNG format works best.

---

### 2. School Events & Celebrations
📁 **Folder:** `public/images/events/{event-folder}/`
* Event folders: `janmashtami`, `independence-day`, `pratapgarh-trip`, `gk-quiz`, `raksha-bandhan`, `diwali`
* **Custom Event Cover:** Simply name an image `cover.png` or `cover.jpg` inside that event folder, and it will automatically become the main cover card and page banner for that event!
* **Album Photos:** Number them sequentially: `1.jpg`, `2.jpg`, `3.jpg`, etc. (or `.jpeg`, `.png`).

---

### 3. About Us Photos
📁 **Folder:** `public/images/about/`
* `about-page.png` — Shown on the dedicated About Us page (`#about`).
* `about.png` — Shown on the homepage About section (`#home`).

---

### 4. Hero & Banner Photo
📁 **Folder:** `public/images/hero/`
* **File name:** `hero.jpg` (or `.png`)
* **Usage:** Main big photo on the homepage banner next to the sun & clouds.

---

### 4. General Gallery / Campus Photos
📁 **Folder:** `public/images/gallery/`
* Any additional school campus, playground, or student activity photos.
* E.g.: `campus-1.jpg`, `playground.jpg`, `library.jpg`.

---

### How to upload in Windows:
1. Open File Explorer on your computer.
2. Go to:
   `c:\Users\Sweety\Downloads\mv_scholars_preschool_starter\public\images\`
3. Open any folder (`logo`, `events`, `hero`, `gallery`) and paste your images there.
4. Refresh your browser (`http://localhost:5173/`) to see your images live!
